About AVEN

A Smarter Way to Stay Connected

Powering people, one connection at a time.

Never let a dead battery slow you down.

In today's fast-moving world, staying connected is no longer a luxury — it's a necessity. Whether you're attending classes, travelling, shopping, working, or enjoying an event, your smartphone and digital devices are always part of your journey.

AVEN is a smart power bank rental service designed to make portable charging accessible, convenient, and effortless.

Through our automated self-service rental kiosks, users can quickly rent a portable charger, power up their devices while on the move, and return the power bank at a participating nearby AVEN station.

We aim to establish a convenient charging network across high-footfall locations, including airports, railway stations, shopping malls, colleges, universities, corporate offices, cafés, public spaces, and events.

Our technology-driven approach combines convenience, accessibility, and smart infrastructure to create a seamless charging experience.

At AVEN, we believe that running out of battery should never mean disconnecting from what matters.

Our Mission

To make reliable portable charging accessible to everyone through an affordable, technology-driven power bank rental network.

Our Vision

To build a connected ecosystem of smart charging stations across India, making on-the-go charging as accessible and convenient as everyday digital payments.

Our Promise

Simple rentals. Convenient returns. Reliable connectivity.

How AVEN Works

Charging Made Simple

Getting portable power has never been easier.

01

Step 01

Find a Station

Locate a nearby AVEN smart power bank rental kiosk at a participating location.

02

Step 02

Scan and Rent

Scan the QR code displayed on the kiosk and follow the instructions to initiate your rental.

03

Step 03

Take Your Power Bank

Once your rental is confirmed, the kiosk releases a portable power bank. Take it with you and charge your compatible devices.

04

Step 04

Stay Connected

Use your rented power bank while travelling, studying, working, shopping, or attending events.

05

Step 05

Return with Ease

After use, return the power bank to an available participating AVEN kiosk according to the rental terms.

That's it! No complicated process. Just convenient charging whenever you need it.
